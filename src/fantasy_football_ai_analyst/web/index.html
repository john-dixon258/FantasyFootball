A LITTLE CONTEXT

Know your numbers.

Useful statistics start with understanding what they measure.

Total points
FPL points recorded so far in your saved dataset.
Points per £m
Season points divided by the player's current price in millions. A measure of observed value, not a forecast or historical return on investment.
Points per 90
Season points × 90 ÷ minutes played. Small samples can look impressive; use a minimum-minutes filter for context.
Next opponent
The first unstarted, unfinished fixture in your saved fixtures. Undated matches follow scheduled matches. A dash means the opponent is unknown.
Your shortlist
Starred player IDs are saved in this browser on this device. They are not an account or a submitted FPL team. Clear old selections when switching seasons.
ABOUT THIS SNAPSHOT

This app reads the files you have saved. File save times show when those local files changed, not when FPL last updated them. There is no live data feed or automatic freshness check.
